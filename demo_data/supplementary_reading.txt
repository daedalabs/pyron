PACELC THEOREM EXTENSION:
Proposed by Daniel Abadi in 2012. It extends CAP by stating:
- If there is a Partition (P), how does the system trade off Availability (A) and Consistency (C)?
- Else (E), when the system is running normally without partitions, how does it trade off Latency (L) and Consistency (C)?
Example: MongoDB is PC/EC (prefers consistency in both states), while Cassandra is PA/EL (prefers availability and low latency).

RAFT LEADER ELECTION RULES:
1. Node votes for at most one candidate in a given term (First-come-first-served).
2. Candidate's log must be at least as up-to-date as the voter's log (Log completeness property).
3. If election timeout elapses without a leader or candidate receiving a majority, a new term begins with fresh randomized timeouts.
